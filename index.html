About

I am a doctoral researcher in applied mathematics at the University of Oulu. I develop methods to recover hidden properties of a medium from measurements taken at its boundary or far away, from proofs that the reconstruction is possible to numerical methods that carry it out.

Research interests: inverse problems, nonlinear PDEs, inverse scattering, numerical reconstruction methods, scientific and high-performance computing.
